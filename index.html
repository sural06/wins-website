What we do

Three things, done well

  • Briefings & speaker series

    Closed-door conversations with people who've worked inside the Pentagon, the intelligence community, and the National Security Council — the kind of career detail that doesn't make it into a panel Q&A.

  • Mentorship

    First-years and sophomores are paired with upperclass members and alumnae working across defense, intelligence, and foreign policy, for advice on coursework, internships, and clearance timelines.

  • Community

    A standing group of women who take the subject seriously and are direct with each other about the path — case discussions, application seasons, and the ordinary parts of building a career in this field.
